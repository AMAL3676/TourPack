Detailed Day-Wise Plan

Day 1: Jaipur (Arrival)

Arrival at Jaipur, check-in to your heritage hotel. Evening: Visit Nahargarh Fort for a magnificent sunset view over the Pink City skyline.

Day 2: Jaipur Sightseeing

Explore Amer Fort (including an Elephant ride), a photo-stop at the iconic Hawa Mahal, and guided tours of the City Palace and Jantar Mantar observatory.

Day 3: Jodhpur (via Pushkar Stop)

Morning drive to Jodhpur. En-route: Visit Pushkar to see the Holy Lake and the famous Brahma Temple. Continue to Jodhpur and check into your hotel.

Day 4: Jodhpur Sightseeing

Full Day: Visit the majestic Mehrangarh Fort, pay respects at Jaswant Thada. Evening: Explore the unique blue alleys and markets of the Blue City.

Day 5: Udaipur (The City of Lakes)

Scenic morning drive to Udaipur. Check-in and relax. Evening: Enjoy a serene sunset boat ride on Lake Pichola, viewing the Jag Mandir.

Day 6: Udaipur Sightseeing

Explore the grand City Palace Udaipur, the beautiful Saheliyon Ki Bari (Garden of the Maidens), and finish the day with local market exploration.

Day 7: Jaipur (Return Journey)

Morning drive back to Jaipur (or nearby accommodation). Spend the evening at leisure for rest or last-minute souvenir shopping.

Day 8: Departure

Check out from the hotel and private transfer to Jaipur International Airport (JAI) or Railway Station for your onward journey.
